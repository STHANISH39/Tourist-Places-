Pykara Falls

Pykara Falls, located in Ooty, Tamil Nadu, is a popular tourist destination known for its
scenic beauty and cascading waterfalls.
1. Cascading Waterfalls:
Pykara Falls is formed by the Pykara River as it descends in a series of cascades, with the
last two falls being the most prominent.
2. Sacred River:
The Pykara River is considered sacred by the Toda tribe, adding cultural significance to the area.
3. Hydroelectric Project:
The river also plays a role in an important hydroelectric power project, with a dam and
power station located nearby.
4. Boating:
Pykara Lake, formed by the dam, offers boating activities, making it a popular spot for recreation.
5. Picturesque Setting:
The falls and surrounding landscape, including the vast grassy meadows of Wenlock Downs,
provide a scenic backdrop for photography and picnics.
6. Tourist Attraction:
Pykara Falls is a well-known tourist spot, attracting visitors with its natural beauty
and recreational opportunities.

Sadras Beach

Sadras Beach, near Mahabalipuram in Tamil Nadu, is known for its serene ambiance, historical fort, and Dutch cemetery, offering a tranquil escape with a touch of history. It's a picturesque location with casuarina groves, colorful fishing boats, and opportunities for relaxation and exploration.
1. Historical Significance:
Sadras Beach is home to a well-preserved Dutch fort and cemetery, remnants of the Dutch East India Company's presence in the area.
2. Secluded and Tranquil:
The beach is known for its peaceful atmosphere, offering a quieter alternative to more crowded beaches.
3. Scenic Beauty:
Sadras Beach is surrounded by casuarina groves, providing shade and a picturesque backdrop for relaxation and photography.
4. Dutch Cemetery:
The Dutch cemetery features tombstones with inscriptions dating back to the 17th and 18th centuries, offering a glimpse into the past.
5. Proximity to Mahabalipuram:
Sadras is located near Mahabalipuram, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its ancient temples and rock-cut sculptures, making it an easy addition to a historical or cultural itinerary.

Siruvani Waterfalls

Siruvani Waterfalls, located 36 km west of Coimbatore, is famous for its sweet water and
scenic beauty. It's a popular destination for nature lovers, offering trekking, picnicking, and
birdwatching opportunities. The falls are accessible via a 1 km trek through the forest after
a drive through the Sadivayal Checkpost.
1. Sweet Water:
The water of Siruvani is renowned for its unique, sweet taste, attributed to its low mineral
content and high oxygen levels.
2. Scenic Beauty:
The waterfall is nestled amidst the lush greenery of the Western Ghats, offering breathtaking
views and a tranquil environment.
3. Accessibility:
The main access point is through the Sadivayal Checkpost, where an entry fee is charged. Private
vehicles are not permitted beyond this point, and visitors need to use forest department vehicles
to reach the trekking point.
4. Trekking and Activities:
A 1 km trek through the forest is required to reach the bathing spot at the falls.
Popular activities include trekking, nature walks, bird watching, and picnicking.
5. Best Time to Visit:
The ideal time to visit is after the monsoon season when the waterfall is at its fullest
and the surroundings are vibrant.

Silver Cascade Falls

Silver Cascade Falls in Kodaikanal is a popular tourist spot known for its picturesque, cascading waterfall and the surrounding natural beauty. It's an ideal location for a short break during a trip, offering scenic views and opportunities to enjoy local snacks and refreshments.
1. Artificial Waterfall:
The falls are formed from the excess water of the man-made Kodaikanal Lake, making them an artificial cascade.
2. Scenic Location:
Situated on the way to Kodaikanal from Madurai, it's a convenient stop for travelers and offers a refreshing break.
3. Photogenic Spot:
The waterfall's silvery appearance, especially when viewed during the rainy season, makes it a popular spot for photography.
4. Refreshment Stalls:
Numerous street food vendors and shops are located near the falls, offering snacks like roasted corn, fresh coconut water, and local jewelry.
5. Proximity to Other Attractions:
It's located near other popular Kodaikanal attractions, such as Kodaikanal Lake, Coaker's Walk, and Bryant Park.

Covelong Beach

Covelong Beach, also known as Kovalam Beach, is a popular destination in Tamil Nadu,
India, known for its beautiful coastline, water sports, and historical significance. It's located
40km from Chennai and is a charming fishing village with a vibrant atmosphere.
1. Location:
Situated on the Coromandel Coast along the Bay of Bengal, Covelong Beach is a coastal
suburb and fishing village.
2. History:
Covelong was once a port town established by the Nawab of Carnatic and later developed by
the Dutch who built a fort.
3. Activities:
The beach is a hub for water sports like windsurfing, kayaking, and canoeing, with a dedicated
surfing school.
4. Historical Significance:
Remains of the Dutch fort and an ancient Catholic Church are notable historical landmarks
near the beach.
5. Fishing Village:
The adjacent village of Covelong is a fishing village, and the local fishermen are often
willing to take visitors on fishing trips.

Catherine Falls

Catherine Falls, located near Kotagiri in Tamil Nadu, is a double-cascaded waterfall known for its scenic beauty and trekking opportunities. It's a popular spot for tourists visiting the Ooty-Coonoor area. The falls are surrounded by lush greenery, tea plantations, and the Nilgiri forests.
1. Double Cascades:
Catherine Falls is a two-tiered waterfall, with the upper falls being fed by the Kallar River.
2. Location and Accessibility:
Situated on the Mettupalayam Road, branching off at Aravenu, it's accessible from Kotagiri. The falls can be viewed from Dolphin's Nose, a nearby viewpoint.
3. Trekking Opportunities:
Catherine Falls offers trekking routes through the forest, including a popular trek from Aravenu and a longer trek through Longwood Shola Forest (with permission).
4. Surrounding Scenery:
The falls are surrounded by picturesque Nilgiri forests, tea plantations, and offer views of the Mettupalayam plains.
5. Named After a Planter's Wife:
The waterfall is named after Catherine, the wife of M.D. Cockburn, a Scottish planter who introduced coffee and other crops to the region.

Emerald Lake

Emerald Lake is known for its stunning emerald green waters and is a popular destination for
nature lovers, photographers, and birdwatchers. It's surrounded by lush green hills and tea
gardens, offering a tranquil escape. The lake is part of the Kundah Hydro Power Project and
is a significant part of the local ecosystem.
1. Scenic Beauty:
The lake's most striking feature is its vibrant emerald green water, reflecting the surrounding
hills and sky. This creates a picturesque and serene environment.
2. Biodiversity:
Emerald Lake is rich in flora and fauna, making it a great spot for nature enthusiasts.
Birdwatchers can observe various bird species, including migratory birds.
3. Trekking and Photography:
The surrounding hills offer trekking trails for all levels, providing panoramic views of the
lake and its surroundings. The landscape also provides ample opportunities for photography,
especially at sunrise and sunset.
4. Tea Gardens:
Emerald Lake is surrounded by tea plantations, offering visitors a chance to explore the
tea gardens, learn about tea processing, and enjoy a fresh cup of tea.
5. Tranquility and Relaxation:
Emerald Lake is a peaceful destination where visitors can relax, enjoy the natural beauty,
and escape the hustle and bustle of city life.

Elk Falls

Elk Falls, near Kotagiri in Tamil Nadu, is a scenic waterfall known for its beauty and historical connection to the British colonial era. It's a popular spot for tourists, offering views of the Kookal Valley and surrounding tea and coffee plantations.
1. Tourist Destination:
Elk Falls is a popular tourist destination, attracting visitors with its natural beauty and historical charm. 2. Waterfall:
It's a picturesque waterfall, known for its gushing water, especially during the monsoon season.
3. Kookal Valley:
The falls offer a view of the Kookal Valley, known for its orange cultivation, which is particularly vibrant during the monsoon.
4. British Influence:
A European house, built during the British colonial period (likely by British collector Sullivan), is located near the falls, adding to its historical significance.
5. Best Time to Visit:
The monsoon season (June to September) is considered the best time to visit, as the waterfall is in full flow and the Kookal Valley is at its most scenic.
6. Nearby Attractions:
Other attractions in the vicinity include Catherine Falls, Kodanad View Point, Rangaswamy Peak and Pillar, and the John Sullivan Memorial.

Elliot's Beach

Elliot's Beach, also known as Besant Nagar Beach, is a popular Chennai beach known for
its cleanliness, tranquility, and proximity to various attractions. It's a great spot for relaxation,
strolling, and enjoying the sea. Key features include the Karl Schmidt Memorial and nearby
temples like Ashtalakshmi Temple and Velankanni Church.
1. Cleanliness and Tranquility:
Compared to Marina Beach, Elliot's Beach is generally considered cleaner and less crowded,
offering a more peaceful atmosphere for visitors.
2. Prominent Landmarks:
The beach is home to the Karl Schmidt Memorial, a monument honoring a Dutch sailor
who died saving a person from drowning. The nearby Ashtalakshmi Temple and Velankanni
Church also add to the area's appeal.
3. Variety of Activities:
Visitors can enjoy leisurely walks on the sand, relax by the sea, or partake in activities
like horse riding and enjoying refreshments from nearby stalls and cafes.
4. Accessibility and Parking:
Elliot's Beach is easily accessible by road and has parking facilities available for
those traveling by car.
5. Popular with Locals and Tourists:
The beach is a popular destination for both locals and tourists, offering a relaxing seaside
experience with opportunities to enjoy the beach, food, and nearby religious and cultural sites.

Bear Shola Falls

Bear Shola Falls, a serene and picturesque waterfall near Kodaikanal, is known for its tranquil setting amidst lush forests and its name, which originates from the bears that used to frequent the area.
1. Seasonal Attraction:
Bear Shola Falls is a seasonal waterfall, meaning it's most impressive during the monsoon season, typically from September to December, when the water flow is at its peak.
2. Tranquil Environment:
The falls are nestled within dense forests, offering a peaceful and serene atmosphere, ideal for relaxation and enjoying nature's sounds, like the chirping of birds.
3. Biodiversity Hotspot:
The surrounding forests are rich in biodiversity, making it a great spot for nature lovers to observe various plant and animal species.
4. Historical Significance:
The falls are named "Bear Shola" because bears used to visit the area to drink water, adding to the falls' unique charm.
5. Accessibility:
Bear Shola Falls is easily accessible, located just a few kilometers from Kodaikanal Lake and the bus station, making it a convenient addition to any Kodaikanal itinerary.
